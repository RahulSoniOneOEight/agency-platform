# Client Review

## PURPOSE
Convert client evaluation of A/B/C prototypes into one approved implementation contract.

## READ
- `client-projects/<client>/directions/`
- runnable prototypes
- direction comparison
- QA findings
- client feedback
- `templates/approved-experience.yaml`

## PROCESS
1. Present rationale, target user/job, strengths, trade-offs, and working prototype for each direction.
2. Capture select-one, mix, reject, and refine decisions.
3. Resolve mixed selections into one internally coherent experience contract.

## WRITE
- `client-projects/<client>/approved-experience.yaml`

## VALIDATE
All selected components, patterns, journeys, and resources must exist and the client approval state must be explicit.

## DO NOT
- Do not treat meeting notes as the production contract.
- Do not productionize unapproved alternatives.
